Morning routine — face only (steps 3–6)

Four steps after hair steps 1–2. Order matters: vitamin C under essence under sunscreen.

Face sequence

  1. La Roche-Posay Toleriane Hydrating Gentle Cleanser — face.
  2. Timeless Vitamin C 20% — replace when yellow-orange.
  3. Aestura Atobarrier 365 Hydro Essence
  4. La Roche-Posay Anthelios Clear Skin SPF 60 — daily.

Night A — regeneration (face only)

Alternate with Night B. Focus: cellular communication and light energy. Steps 1–4 below; step 5 (Problend) is on Hair care.

Face sequence

  1. EltaMD Skin Recovery Cleanser
  2. Plated Skin Science Intense Serum — apply to clean, bare skin.
  3. CurrentBody Skin Series 2 LED Mask — 10–15 minutes immediately after serum.
  4. Aestura Atobarrier 365 Cream — generous layer for barrier repair.

Night B — remodeling (face only)

Focus: collagen turnover and overnight recovery. Steps 1–3 below; step 4 (Problend) is on Hair care.

Face sequence

  1. EltaMD Skin Recovery Cleanser
  2. Adapalene gel or Altreno 0.05% (tretinoin) — pea-sized amount, entire face. Avoid sensitive skin directly around eyes and mouth corners. On nights when you also use scalp tretinoin (via Problend), keep this product below the upper forehead — see hairline interface.
  3. Mitopure Barrier Cream — 15 minutes after retinoid.
Cadence Alternating A and B puts the retinoid at three to four nights weekly — sustainable and captures most available benefit. Work toward more frequent use if skin tolerates.

Hairline interface (not hair products)

On Night B, facial retinoid and scalp tretinoin (in Problend — see Hair care) can meet at the upper forehead. This is a placement rule for your face routine, not a hair treatment step.

Night B — upper forehead band If you see irritation, peeling, or redness along the upper forehead, keep the facial retinoid below that line rather than reducing scalp Rx. Minoxidil timing and pillow safety are covered on Hair care.
What is genuinely well built Sequencing is correct — vitamin C under moisturizer under sunscreen, actives separated across alternating nights, barrier support taken seriously, retinoid buffered with a wait before cream. Cleansers are appropriately gentle. Nothing on this page is doing damage.

Post-operative rules — face only

Face only — below the eyebrows During acute recovery, keep all facial skincare below the eyebrows. Do not apply face products to the scalp, donor zone, or recipient area until Dr. Sean clears you. Scalp washing and Rx holds: Hair care peri-op · Transplant.
  • Pre-op Pause facial retinoid ~1 week before surgery. Scalp minoxidil / Problend stops are on Hair care and Transplant.
  • Days 0–14 Facial skincare below eyebrows only. No LED, exosomes, or retinoids on face until cleared.
  • Weeks 4+ Plated exosomes and LED more defensible once barrier is intact. Restart facial retinoid when skin tolerates; scalp Rx only when Dr. Sean clears.
