Care

What to expect as grafts settle. Dr. Sean’s post-op video still wins on washing and scabs.

Days 0–14

Protect & wash

Follow the clinic’s ~25-minute post-op video for washing and scab care. Elevated sleep, Tylenol only, baby shampoo — no ibuprofen.

  • Scabbing is normal for about two weeks
  • Keep grafts protected; button-front shirts only
  • High protein + whey concentrate
First month

Shock loss

Transplanted hairs typically shed within the first month. Native hair around recipient sites can shed too. Looking worse before better is expected — not graft failure.

~Week 4 · mid-Sep

Restart Problend

Resume Happy Head Problend while staying on oral finasteride. No swimming or hair dye until this window clears. Microneedling only after explicit clearance.

Months 2–4

Quiet, then wisps

Months 2–3 are quiet. Around months 3–4, fine regrowth starts — often lighter and softer than the final result.

Months 6–12

Maturation

Month 6 is roughly half the result. Month 12 is about 80–90% — the earliest fair point to judge. Crown finishes last through months 12–18.

Ongoing

Keep native hair

Long-term outcome depends on stopping native loss. Stay on antiandrogen coverage (finasteride / Problend) after the peri-op bridge.
