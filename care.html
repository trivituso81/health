First night · critical

Do not bump your head

Car doors and cabinet doors are the usual culprits. Keep the spray on mist, ~1–2 inches from grafts, every 10 minutes while awake. Refrigerate the spray. Set alarms for 2am and 4am unless you’re exhausted or returning for day-two surgery.

  • Sleep at 45° with a neck pillow if frontal work was done
  • Drink water or juice every hour until bedtime; pee before sleep
  • Eat a solid dinner; take antibiotic when you get home
  • Tylenol 500 mg ×2 at home; repeat every 8 hours if needed (max 4,000 mg/day) — no alcohol with Tylenol
  • Compression socks on; sit when using the restroom for 24 hours
  • No heavy lifting, sweating, or bending at the waist — squat to keep the head level
Lightheaded · very common

Head down, knees bent

After a long procedure, blood pools in the legs. If you feel lightheaded, nauseous, or faint: immediately put your head low and bend your knees until it passes. Do not rush to the bathroom. Get out of bed in steps — sit at the edge, drink, stand slowly, wait 1–2 minutes.

Bleeding · rare

Firm pressure 20–30 min

Apply steady pressure to the donor bleed; repeat if needed. Call Dr. Sean. If it continues, ice-pack pressure on the exact point. Out of town and still bleeding → ER for a suture; attending can call 310-237-8760 any hour.

Day-by-day

Day 1 · Thu 8/20

Spray · tape · ACell

  • Spray every 20 min; napkin on forehead so the bandage stays dry
  • Keep forehead tape on (4 days/nights cuts peri-orbital swelling)
  • Ice forehead 3×/day, 5–10 min — never on grafts. Massage swelling from mid-forehead out to temples, a few times an hour
  • Remove donor bandage midday; apply ACell 1 Tbsp at 8am / 12pm / 5pm, re-wrap with Kerlix; ~2 hours later add lots of Vaseline over ACell (skip Vaseline if you have two ACell jars)
  • Rub ACell against the grain (bottom → top) into the holes as tenderness allows
  • AM: aspirin, pentoxifylline, one Cephalexin, full prednisone · PM: Cephalexin + half oral minoxidil; last night at 45°
  • No recipient or donor wash required yet
Day 2 · Fri 8/21

Advil OK · first rinse

  • Spray every 30 min; continue massage / ice / ACell ×3; same AM/PM clinic Rx (aspirin · pentox · cephalexin · full prednisone / cephalexin · ½ minoxidil)
  • NSAIDs OK now — e.g. 2 Advil + 2 Tylenol every 8 hours with food if needed
  • Compression socks, 45° sleep, and 2-step exit optional from here
  • Start cup-rinse of grafts with baby shampoo (no touching) — see Wash
  • Gently wash/massage donor with baby shampoo; don’t scrape ACell off
Days 3–4

Optional spray · keep swelling work

  • Day 3: spray optional every few hours. Day 4: no more spray
  • Massage + ice forehead; ACell ×3; AM full prednisone + morning meds; PM Cephalexin + half minoxidil
  • Cup-rinse grafts every other day; wash donor every other day
  • Itching is nerves waking up — light spray/pad on recipient; donor may use OTC hydrocortisone 1% BID (ask Dr. Sean if that fails)
Days 5–11

Tape off · finish ACell · antibiotics

  • Day 5: gently remove forehead tape; stop massage/ice; last prednisone; keep ACell ×3 until the jar is empty (~4–5 days per jar)
  • Day 6+: cleanse donor thoroughly with fingertips once ACell is done
  • Antibiotics through ~day 10 (or longer if donor isn’t healed)
  • Days 7+: vertical + horizontal donor squeeze 20 sec, twice daily if you’re prone to ingrowns
  • Daily (or every-other) cup-rinse of grafts; wash donor
Days 12–14+

Scab softener protocol

  • Start day 12 if crust is heavy, or day 14 if mild
  • Baby oil on grafts overnight → morning: baby shampoo + conditioner, sit 20 min → circular massage 4 min → gentle rinse
  • Hairs coming out with scabs is OK — grafts are rooted. Repeat a few days until clear, then stay gentle through day 30
  • No shower pressure on grafts for 4 weeks; blot or air-dry, never rub

Chart 4 · Growth

Month 1

Shed

Transplanted hairs fall out. Shock loss of native hair is uncommon (~4%) and temporary if it happens — protein, vitamins, text Dr. Sean.

Months 2–4

Quiet

Little visible growth. Be patient — clinic says growth typically starts around month 4–5.

Months 5–7

Hairs begin

New growth appears. Ingrowns can feel like acne — finish antibiotics; Dr. Sean can prescribe clindamycin 1% if needed.

Months 8–10

Adequate growth

Density and caliber keep improving. Hair grows ~1 inch every 3 months once it starts.

Months 11–15

Final result

~12 months for a fair read; full maturation by ~15 months. Formal follow-up by month 12; photos to Dr. Sean every 2 months; check-ins at 3 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 15 months (or text monthly).

Donor healing · from day 21

3× per week · after day 21 (from Wed 8/19)

Dermastamp · topical · LED

Start ~21 days after the second procedure day. Apply 0.25 mm dermastamp to donor ~2 min (no bleeding) → topical minoxidil / Problend-class solution → LED red light same day or next. Begin LED + solution first if sensitive, then add stamp. Clean stamp with isopropyl alcohol after each use. Stop if irritated.

  • Office: 10 LED red-light sessions + Prodeep (1340 nm) — call 310-315-4989
  • Recipient acceleration from month 1 onward (not sooner): gentle 0.25 mm stamp 1 min, 3×/week, then topical; optional LaserCap 10 min 3×/week; ask about oral minoxidil and Prodeep 8-week course
