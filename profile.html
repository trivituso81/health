Interpretation

What your labs mean for the current medication, supplement, hair, and longevity stack.

What looks solid Metabolic health (glucose 89–90), liver enzymes (AST/ALT teens), kidney function (eGFR 85–101), lipids on rosuvastatin (LDL 76, triglycerides 65), thyroid (TSH 1.76), PSA (0.49 — excellent baseline if you start finasteride), and a clean infectious screen. You are not supplementing into a hidden problem on these panels.
What needs improvement Nothing here is a pre-op blocker — surgery clearance labs are fine. These are the gaps and soft spots worth addressing after recovery:
  • Androgen axis — most actionable. Free testosterone 9.7 pg/mL (lower third of range) and total T 390 ng/dL (low-normal for 45). Not deficient, but not optimal. A Defy conversation post-op makes sense before stacking more DIY androgen-adjacent supplements (boron, black maca). Mechanism read and sequencing: Peptides & TRT.
  • Ferritin — unknown. Never drawn in any panel. Low ferritin is one of the most common fixable causes of shedding; you cannot rule it in or out. Add at next Defy draw — see Not yet drawn.
  • Vitamin D — dosing blind. You take D3+K2 daily without a 25-OH level. Not dangerous at typical doses, but you cannot optimize. Same next-draw add.
  • Estradiol on the low side (<30 pg/mL, Feb 2026). Not a problem today, but worth rechecking if you add finasteride or pursue TRT — both can shift E2.
  • Creatinine context. 1.09 → 0.95 mg/dL with 5 g creatine daily and olmesartan — likely fine, but always disclose creatine when kidney labs are read. Do not stop creatine pre-op unless a clinician directs it.
  • Optional refinements, not urgent: B12/folate not checked; ApoB/Lp(a) not baselined despite excellent LDL; DHEA-S 128 μg/dL (low-mid for age) — all reasonable next-draw adds if Defy is consolidating panels.
