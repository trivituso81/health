Shampoo & body wash

Morning steps 1–2 — see Morning routine.

Paul Mitchell — reconsider daily use Sulfate-based, menthol-heavy daily use on a scalp receiving nightly minoxidil and tretinoin. Evening Problend helps separate shampoo from actives, but cumulative irritation can still cause shedding. Post-op: use J&J Baby Shampoo per Dr. Sean’s sheet, not Paul Mitchell, until cleared.
Ketoconazole alternative If you have flaking or seborrheic dermatitis, ketoconazole 2% shampoo two or three times weekly is better targeted and has weak supporting data in androgenetic alopecia. Not in your current routine — optional swap if needed.

Jack Black Turbo Wash is Neutral — body wash only, no meaningful interaction with scalp or facial actives.

Minoxidil safety

Apply ≥2 hours before bed Problend goes on both nights. Minoxidil migrating onto the face — by runoff, hand contact, or pillowcase — can cause unwanted facial hair. Apply at least two hours before bed, wash hands thoroughly, use a dedicated pillowcase changed often.
Cat toxicity Minoxidil is severely toxic to cats even in trace amounts. If you have a cat, hand-washing and pillow hygiene are essential.
